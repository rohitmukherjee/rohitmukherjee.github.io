Real estate

Singapore Rental Property Investment Calculator

Aggregates real URA-style condo transaction data into per-project yield tables, computes exact after-tax cash flow with Singapore's real BSD, ABSD, and progressive-tax rules, and overlays a stochastic price-rent model that flags whether current prices look stretched relative to rents. Covers 7 central districts, ~17k sales and ~75k rental transactions.
